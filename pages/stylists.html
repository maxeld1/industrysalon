Meet the Team

Mary Beth

Mary Beth is a passionate hairstylist, and now salon owner with over 20 years of experience in the industry. For her, hair has always been more than a career, it’s a way to build trust, create connection, and help clients feel confident as the best version of themselves. She specializes in customized color and effortless, lived-in styles, with a strong focus on detail. After years behind the chair, Mary Beth took a leap of faith to create a salon space that reflects what she values most: a relaxed, welcoming environment rooted in quality, continued education, and genuine care. Every appointment is about more than great hair, it’s about confidence, comfort, and feeling truly seen.

Lisa

Lisa has always been inspired by the energy of a salon. The creativity, the community, and the stylists who seemed so invested into their work. She knew from a young age that this industry was where she belonged. Lisa started in a salon at 15, shampooing and training, and now with 18 years in the industry and 14 years behind the chair, her passion for learning has only grown. Education is at the heart of everything she does. She loves expanding her skills, trying new techniques, and bringing fresh ideas back to her clients. Her signature style is hard to define, but close to perfection. Lisa customizes every look to the individual—no two guests are the same, and that’s what keeps her excited to be behind the chair for all this time 🤍

Sam

With nearly 11 years in the beauty industry, Sam began her journey as a shampoo assistant and has spent the past nine years refining her craft behind the chair. She is deeply passionate about building meaningful connections with her clients and helping them feel like the best versions of themselves—inside and out. While she offers a wide range of services, her true creative passion lies in the art of hair color. She finds great fulfillment in transforming not only hair, but also the confidence of each client she works with. A strong believer in continued education, Sam is always striving to grow, learn, and stay at the forefront of industry trends and techniques. She is thrilled to begin this new chapter at Industry Salon, working alongside a team of talented women she trusts and admires. Together, they are committed to creating a welcoming, elevated environment where every client feels valued, confident, and beautiful.

Ali

Ali is focused on growing her career, while loving what she does, making people feel beautiful. Always open to learning new things and being a team player, Ali works hard to create a comfortable, welcoming vibe where clients can relax, trust the process, and leave the chair feeling confident, refreshed, and happy.

Bri

Bri has over 10 years of experience in the hair industry and specializes in all aspects of hair, including color, cutting, and formal styling. Her true passion lies in blonding services and creating dimensional, lived-in, and natural-looking color. Bri is certified in hand-tied extensions and loves helping clients achieve their dream hair by adding length, fullness, and confidence—especially for those with finer hair. She is dedicated to making every client look and feel their absolute best.

Brianna

She knew she wanted to do hair from the time she was little, and turning that dream into reality has been incredibly rewarding. Loving what she does every day, she takes pride in helping her clients feel confident and beautiful through her work.

Maria

Leia

Leia was born and raised right here in our community, Leia now lives locally with her husband and three amazing children. With a strong foundation in cutting, coloring, and styling, Leia is known for her keen eye for detail and dedication to quality. She’s passionate about helping every client feel confident, beautiful, and truly their best. From behind the chair to on-site bridal, Leia’s experience and heart for her craft make her such a special part of The Industry team.
